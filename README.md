## 🚀 About OpenCourse
OpenCourse is an **AI-driven platform** that creates **personalized learning paths** for students and professionals.  
Our mission: **make quality education accessible for everyone, everywhere.**

🌐 [Visit Website](https://www.opencourse.in)  

---

## ✨ Features
- 🤖 **AI-Powered Learning** – Smart, personalized course recommendations  
- ⚡ **Next.js Framework** – Blazing-fast, SEO-friendly frontend  
- 🎨 **Customizable Courses** – Tailored to individual learning goals  
- 🌍 **Inclusive Education** – Focus on accessibility and affordability  
- 🔓 **Open-Source Inspired** – Transparent & community-driven development
